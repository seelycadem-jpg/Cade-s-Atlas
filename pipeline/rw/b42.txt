## na202197 | 2021 | Jan 6 Capitol attack | who: Trump supporters | where: Washington DC
  bluf: The January 6 attack on the Capitol was an attempt by Trump supporters to stop Congress from certifying the 2020 election result. It failed, but it became a defining event in American debates over the peaceful transfer of power and political violence.
  now: It remains a defining event in debates over the peaceful transfer of power, and Americans are deeply divided over how to remember it.
  story: On January 6, 2021, as Congress met to certify Joe Biden's election victory, President Donald Trump told a rally near the White House that the election had been stolen and urged the crowd to march to the Capitol, to make their voices heard 'peacefully and patriotically' but also to 'fight like hell'. Thousands broke through police lines and into the building, forcing lawmakers to flee; about 140 police officers were injured. Congress completed the certification that night.

  then: More than 1,500 people were charged, and Trump was impeached a second time and acquitted by the Senate; on returning to office in 2025 he pardoned or commuted the sentences of nearly all of them.
