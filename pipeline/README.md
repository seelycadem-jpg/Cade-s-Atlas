# Pipeline: the build scripts behind Prototype 1

Recovered on 5 November 2026 from the workspace of the main atlas chat, where they ran. This folder holds every script, the research inputs they read, the writing briefs, and the map intermediates needed to rerun the last map step.

**Layout.** This folder mirrors the old workspace root, `/home/claude`. Built outputs went to `/home/claude/site/` (now this repo's `site/`) and `/home/claude/atlas/maps/` (now `site/maps/`). Scratch files went to `/tmp`. Paths are hard-coded throughout the scripts: the first refactoring job is one config that points them at this repo (`pipeline/` for code, `site/` for outputs, a git-ignored `work/` for scratch).

**What is here and what is not.**
- Here: all scripts, `review/` (border corrections, gazetteer, control points, the 690 ancient events, briefs, the writing pipeline), the front-end source of Prototype 1 (`atlas/`), the original timeline script (`uploads/build.py`), and the map intermediates `data/g4/own_base.json`, `own_anc.json`, `nb.json`.
- Not here: the raw third-party sources (download them; table below) and two large intermediates. `prov.geojson` (32 MB, the province mesh) is regenerated by `g4/prov.py` and `g4/prov_rivers.py` from Natural Earth; both are seeded, so the same Natural Earth files should give the same mesh: they came from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector at commit ca96624 (2 June 2022), `geojson/` folder. Check the province count (11,203) after the run. `/tmp/g3/snapsF2` (776 MB of corrected period maps, 1400–1885) is not needed while `own_base.json` stands as the frozen record.

**Verified before commit:** `build.sh` rebuilt `site/data.json` and `site/graph.json` byte-identical to this repo's copies; `g4/estimate.py` rebuilt `own.json` matching on all but 3 of 253,502 records (see below). Every map file in `site/maps/` is identical to the workspace's.

## 2. The map pipeline (borders → `atlas/maps/`)

The live globe draws a fixed mesh of 11,203 provinces and colours each by its owner, year by year. Files the app reads: `prov_p.json` (fills), `prov_e.json` (edges), `prov_meta.json`, `prov.json` (topology), `own.json` (owners), plus `land*.json`, `phys*.json`, `cities.json`, `relief*`, `coast.png`. The older layers (`w*.json`, `a*.json`, `g/` chunks, `cs*.json`, `pieces.json`, `changes.json`, `islands.json`) belong to earlier renderers; the app still references some as fallbacks.

### Current chain: `g4/`

| Step | Script | Reads | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `g4/prov.py` | Natural Earth 10m admin-0 and admin-1 (`/tmp/nev/geojson/`) | `/tmp/g4/prov.geojson`: the province mesh |
| 2 | `g4/prov_rivers.py` | the mesh | splits desert-river valleys (Nile, Tigris-Euphrates, Indus…) into valley strips; keeps `prov_base.geojson` |
| 3 | `KEEP=12% python3 g4/topo.py` | the mesh (via mapshaper) | `atlas/maps/prov.json`, `prov_p.json`, `prov_e.json`, `prov_meta.json`; `/tmp/g4/nb.json` (neighbours) |
| 4 | `g4/owners.py` | the mesh; corrected period maps `/tmp/g3/snapsF2/` (1400–1885) and `/tmp/g3/spans_f.json`; CShapes 2.0 `/tmp/rv/cs.geojson` and `/tmp/rv/csgaps.geojson` (1886–2019) | `/tmp/g4/own_base.json` |
| 5 | `g4/owners_anc.py` | the mesh; Cliopatria v0.2.1 (`/tmp/clio/cliopatria_polities_only_v021.geojson`); historical-basemaps world maps (`/tmp/hb/geojson/world_*.geojson`) | `/tmp/g4/own_anc.json` (3400 BCE–1399) |
| 6 | `g4/estimate.py` | `own_base.json`, `own_anc.json`, `nb.json`, the research inputs below | `atlas/maps/own.json` |
| — | `g4/audit.py` | `own.json` | `audit.py checks` (flicker, jumps, anachronisms); `audit.py dump REGION OUT` (decade snapshots for human or agent review) |

`estimate.py` applies, in order: box-bounded renames, box-only clears, three fill passes, the corrections in `review/fix/*.json` (options `peoples`, `only_open`, `at`), and the native-statehood test. Research inputs:
- `review/gaz/`: a gazetteer of polities and their researched extents, used to fill land the maps leave empty;
- `review/cp/`: research control points (who ruled a capital in a given year);
- `review/fix/`: region-by-region corrections from the border reviews (`zz_*` files are the latest passes, including the ancient ones).

The method, the decisions and the open review items are in the project doc `claude/border-review.md`.

**Time grid in `own.json`:** −3400 to −775 every 25 years, −753, −750 to −10 every 10, 1, 10 to 1390 every 10, then every year 1400–2019. BCE years are negative. Structure: `{pol: [[src, name, overlord, union, extra], ...], prov: {id: [[year, polIndex], ...]}}`.

**Reproducibility, checked 5 Nov 2026:** rerunning `estimate.py` from the shipped intermediates takes about 2.5 minutes and matches the live `own.json` on all but 3 of 253,502 change records (two remote provinces of nomad and Arctic peoples' land differ by one step). The cause is unseeded tie-breaking; fix it by sorting before any set or dict iteration that picks a winner.

**What ships to rerun from step 6:** `own_base.json`, `own_anc.json` and `nb.json` in `data/g4/`; regenerate `prov.geojson` with steps 1–2. Steps 4–5 need the raw sources, and step 4 also needs `/tmp/g3/snapsF2` (776 MB, not shipped; see below).

**Known traps:**
- `pkill -f "python3 estimate.py"` kills its own shell (the pattern matches the shell). Stop runs by PID.
- Running several region dumps in parallel ran out of memory; run them one at a time.
- Province fills can self-intersect after the edge roughening; `topo.py` repairs them with `buffer(0)` before writing `prov_p.json`. Keep that step, or the phone shows shards.

### Upstream history: `g2/`, `g3/`, `mapfix.py`

These built the period maps that `g4/owners.py` samples:
- `mapfix.py` patches historical-basemaps survey maps (1400–2010) by rule into `atlas/maps/w*.json`.
- `g2/build_maps.sh` runs the old chain end to end: land, despiking, gap fills, the shared-edge topology, cities, physical features, islands, dated transfers 1400–1885 and the CShapes ledger.
- `g3/*.py` (prep, snap, corrections, gazfill, fillhb, retime, chunk) produced `/tmp/g3/snapsF2`, the corrected yearly snapshots 1400–1885.

Rebuilding `snapsF2` from scratch means rerunning the g2/g3 chain from the raw sources. The cheaper route is to keep `own_base.json` as the frozen record for 1400–2019 and change owners only through `review/fix/` corrections in `estimate.py`, which is how all recent work was done.

## 3. The front end (`atlas/`)

`atlas/build.py` concatenates `page.html` + `app.css` + `maplibre-gl.css` + `app.js` (with `globe.js`, `globegl.js`, `globeprov.js` inlined) into `atlas/index.html`, copies the `site/*.json` files beside it, and writes a local test copy. `app.js` holds the year slider (weighted segments, BCE years), the ten ages, the world card, the canals overlay and the panels; `globeprov.js` draws the province fills, the edge-based hover outline and same-name realm merging; `globegl.js` the projection switch (globe into mercator) and the canals. The plan is to rebuild this from scratch and keep the data and pipeline.
