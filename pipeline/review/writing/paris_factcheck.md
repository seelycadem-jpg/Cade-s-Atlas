# Fact-check: The Paris Peace Settlement, 1919–1923 (paris_draft.md)

Verified independently against Wikipedia, Britannica, USHMM, CFR, the US State Department (FRUS) and Hungarian press/government pages. The draft's own Sources section was not relied on.

## What must change
1. Binding fix: the guarantee did not lapse on the 49–35 vote; that vote was on Versailles, and the Senate never considered the guarantee treaty. Rewrite the sentence in "Why it held or failed".
2. Tighten: reparations paid → "about 20 billion (estimates 19–21 bn)" and "50 bn in A+B bonds"; mandates (Transjordan within the Palestine mandate, Iraq via 1922 treaty); drop "proportionally most" for "72% of its territory"; reframe the 1.6 m exchange as confirming 1922 flight; optionally note Japan returned Shandong in 1922.
