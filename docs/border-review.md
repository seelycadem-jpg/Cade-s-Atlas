# Meanwhile Atlas: the province map and its review (2026-10-30)

The user asked for an EU4/EU5-style province map, filled everywhere, estimated where unclear and never hand-drawn. The globe is built on that model and now runs from the first cities (3400 BCE) to today.

Province map: 11,203 provinces made once from Natural Earth admin-1 regions (g4/prov.py): small regions merged within a country, large ones split into parts of even size (about 5,000 km2 in old dense cores, 20,000 elsewhere, up to 80,000 in desert and tundra). g4/prov_rivers.py then splits the 301 provinces crossed by desert rivers (Nile, Tigris, Euphrates, Indus, Sutlej, Chenab, Oxus, Jaxartes, Helmand, Tarim, Sahelian Niger and Senegal, Yellow River in the Ordos, Jordan) into the valley strip and the land beyond, so valley states sit on their valleys. Cut lines roughened (g4/topo.py). Files: maps/prov_p.json and maps/prov_e.json (loaded by the map's worker), maps/prov_meta.json, maps/prov.json (topology, loaded only when a state is clicked).

Provinces carry colour only. No line is drawn inside one owner's land; borders appear only where owners differ. Hovering or clicking a realm outlines its outer border and coasts. The globe treats the same name and overlord from different sources as one owner. The globe flattens into a plain (Mercator) map from about 4.5x zoom (projSpec in globegl.js), because phone GPUs dropped triangles of large shapes on the sphere at high zoom.

Time grid: every 25 years from 3400 BCE to Rome's founding (753 BCE), every 10 years to 1390 (no year 0: 1 CE), every year from 1400. The year slider is weighted (3400 BCE-753 BCE 22%, 753 BCE-1400 28%, 1400-2025 50%). The page names the age under the year, with a strip of ages along the slider: Bronze Age (from 3400 BCE), Iron Age (1200 BCE), Classical (500 BCE), Post-classical (500), Exploration (1450), Early Modern (1650), Industrial (1760), World Wars (1914), Atomic (1945), Digital (1991). AGES in app.js. Dates before 700 BCE are flagged approximate.

Owners: maps/own.json gives each province its owner at every grid year (g4/owners.py for 1400+, g4/owners_anc.py for 3400 BCE-1390, then g4/estimate.py). Order of layers:
1. sources: before 1400, Cliopatria polity shapes, then states of the historical-basemaps world map in force (a basemap state lying mostly inside one Cliopatria polity is that polity), then the basemaps' cultures as named peoples' land (CULT regex in owners_anc.py decides culture vs state); 1400-1885 the corrected period maps; 1886-2019 CShapes 2.0 with gaps;
2. clean-up: peoples labels carrying a state's name merged into it; duplicate names merged (never CShapes names); stray far-flung pieces cleared; holes and short flicker removed; renames, some only inside a box (Golden Horde to Great Horde 1466-1502; Small Horde and post-1556 Astrakhan in the Kuban to Lesser Nogai; House of Jagiellon in Ukraine to Grand Duchy of Lithuania; New Netherland in South America to Dutch Republic; Omani Slave Coast to Oman); Iberian Union keeps Portugal's colonies Portuguese;
3. islands join the nearest owner within 4 degrees;
4. estimates from about 2,250 researched polities (review/gaz) on open land;
5. research control points (review/cp);
6. labels valid only inside a box (BOXONLY) cleared outside it;
7. filling (step E): state-named peoples labels to that state within 8 degrees, else nobody; no-record spells up to 25 years split between neighbours in time; pockets of open land up to 12 provinces surrounded by one state go to it;
8. sourced corrections (review/fix/*.json, applied last in file order). Options: owner "peoples:Name"; "only_open": true; "at": [lon, lat] for the single province holding a place. Files: the 1400+ regional reviews; zz_anc_*.json (2026-10-30, about 10,700 corrections from six era reviews, 3400 BCE-1400: africa, americas, east_se_asia, europe_steppe, near_east_med, south_central_asia; brief review/ANC_BRIEF.md); zz_blank_*.json (about 560, brief review/BLANK_BRIEF.md); zz_points.json (Gwadar, Bandar Abbas lease, Socotra); zz_steppe.json;
9. statehood test for the native Americas (step F). A state named only by the period maps (Olmec, Teotihuacan, Moche, Tiwanaku) is treated as state-level.

Statehood test, everywhere: a polity is drawn as a state when it is a state-level society (a ruler or council governing more than one settlement from a fixed seat, with tribute or taxes); cultures, chiefdoms without a seat and pastoral or foraging peoples are named lands in neutral colour. Native Americas after contact: a nation is coloured from the first year a colonial or settler state holds land within about 250 km (the treaty era); Arctic peoples excluded; the PASS list in estimate.py names lordships and paramount chiefdoms that pass on the first test. Era decisions: Cahokia a state 1050-1275, Moundville 1150-1400; Chaco, Chachapoya, Norte Chico, Jenne-jeno, Sao, Toutswe, Nri/Igbo-Ukwu drawn as cultures.

Omani realm: "Yarubid dynasty" (1650-1749), "Oman" for the Al Bu Said realm across Muscat, Zanzibar, Pemba and the Swahili coast until the 1856 split, then "Sultanate of Zanzibar". Kenya's ten-mile coastal strip (1895-1963) not drawn.

Canals: drawn on the overlay (CANALS in globegl.js) only from their opening, Suez 1869 and Panama 1914.

Checks: g4/audit.py checks, dump2 (1400+ decades) and dumpanc (100-year steps to 800 BCE, then 50-year steps to 1350); /tmp/t/blankaudit.py lists open land next to states.

Known limits and open items: estimated extents are approximate; city-states drawn with "at" fill a whole province (about 20,000 km2 outside the cores); the treaty-era rule is a distance proxy. From the era reviews: Hausa states before 1400 unverified; Maya lowlands outside the named cities keep a generic "Mayan City-States" label; Xinjiang and the Red Turban states thin; Arabia before 300 BCE, Iran 900-1000 and the Balkans outside Greece thin; "Austro-Asiatic rice cultures" still covers much of peninsular India c. 2000-1500 BCE; HRE princes as Cliopatria drew them. Post-1400 items flagged: Merina/Madagascar as states at 1400; Māori and Papuans coloured at 1400; Tuʻi Tonga over Fiji; Puebla and Oaxaca 1400-1447 Zaachila labels; "Andean states and chiefdoms" coloured after 1400; Mamprugu shown as Wagadugu; Tamil Nadu 1646-77 and upper Sind 1554-91 gaps.
