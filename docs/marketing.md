# Launch promotion and social media (draft for Cade, 2026-11-16)

A plan to discuss, nothing decided. The facts rule holds everywhere: every post is drawn
from a published page, carries no invented name, date or figure, and every picture has
its source, licence and credit recorded (CLAUDE.md). A map made from Cliopatria data
carries its CC BY credit in the post.

## The idea every post sells

The atlas shows what was happening everywhere at once, and what caused what. Two formats
carry that in a few seconds and belong to no one else:

1. **Meanwhile.** "1492: while Columbus crossed the Atlantic, ..." The globe holds the
   year and the pins light up region by region.
2. **The chain.** "How a treaty signed in 1919 led to ..." Each step of a real graded
   chain appears in turn, with its line and grade word.

## The vibe

The atlas's own look: the warm paper and espresso palette, the red thread for the chain,
the engraved-map feel, EB Garamond titles. Calm and exact in the voice of the pages. The
pull comes from the facts and from seeing the world at once, never from shouting.
Open question: the pages' strict brief register, or a looser voice for social. The
recommendation is the same facts and plain verbs, with shorter sentences and a hook.

## Formats per platform

| Platform | Main format | Also |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (one vertical video serves all three) | 20 to 45 second videos: Meanwhile, the chain, borders changing across a span of years | A voice-over or captions only; test both |
| Instagram feed | Carousels: a chain as five to eight slides, one step per slide | The Meanwhile map of a year as a single image |
| X | The daily game result (spoils nothing), short chain threads, a Meanwhile map | Replies with a page link when history news trends |
| Facebook | History groups and a page; longer posts with one map and a link | Its audience is older and shares more |
| Reddit (proposed addition) | Maps and chains in the history and map communities, by their own rules on self-promotion | Organic only, never paid votes |

## The videos that can travel

All are generated from the atlas's own data, so each costs little once the pipeline
renders it (a step for the build, proposed below):

- **Borders in motion.** The political map of a region moving year by year across a
  span, with the knots appearing as their year passes. The strongest format: map
  timelapses already travel well on every platform.
- **Meanwhile in one year.** The globe turns region to region with one event each.
- **The chain reaction.** Five steps from one event to another, each line drawn in red
  with its grade word.
- **Knot or fibre?** One event, the reader guesses its size, the answer with its reason.
  It doubles as a teaser for the daily game.

## The timeline

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Before launch, 6 weeks | Accounts reserved; three to five videos a week; a sign-up for the email, so launch day has readers waiting |
| Launch week | One video a day; the game opens; a post in each history community where the rules allow |
| After launch | Three to five videos a week; the game result daily on X; each new chronicle gets its own week |
