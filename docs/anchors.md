# Anchor events (started 2026-11-11)

Cade's ratings by judgment. Every later rating is compared with these, and the reach line
is derived from them once about forty are rated across every region and era
(`docs/structure.md`, The method). An anchor is a judgment, so it carries a reason and no
links yet; each is rated in full, with its evidence, when its page is written.

The quick test Cade uses: if the event had never happened, whose story changes? The
world's: a knot. One people's: a fibre, and part of that people's backbone.
