Six ways to mark the selected tab

Each tab has the faint gilt line; the selected one draws its frame in gilt hairline, as a pen would. The selection walks along each row by itself so you can watch the frame draw; tap any tab to try it. Light on the left, dark on the right.

My pick: 1, the cartouche. Second: 5, the scale bar.

The cartouche is the panel an old map puts its title in, so a selected tab in one reads as "this is the part you are reading" without a word, and its notched corners are the only shape here that says atlas rather than website. The scale bar is the quieter choice and the closest to a button. The double rule and the corner marks are handsome but busy at this size; the ribbon is the most playful and the first to tire.
