The fibre mark: every shape on one sheet

The knot is a diamond and the strand a dot; those stay. Every cell shows the three together at reading size and at 12 pixels, light and dark. A note in brown means a map already uses that shape for something, so it would fight the globe. Pick by number.

Shapes left off on purpose: the heart, the arrow (direction is the links' job), the pin (that is the globe's own mark), letters and numerals. Any of these can be made open, filled, or half, and any can be drawn smaller or larger: say the number and the change.
