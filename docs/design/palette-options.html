Meanwhile Atlas · design note, 8 November 2026

Four palettes, light and dark

Each is one set of named colours: the page ground, three inks, a rule, one accent, the four link colours by kind (person, country, chronicle, place), the map's sea, land and border, and six owner fills for the before-and-after maps. The dark side is built from the same set, shifted, never inverted. Every slice is the same piece of page. The numbers under each are contrast ratios; 4.5 is the floor for text, 3 for marks and lines.

The grades keep their line styles in every palette: solid for Necessary and Sufficient, dashed for Contributing, dotted for Contested, grey with a cross-stroke for Coincident. Colour never carries a grade alone, so a reader who cannot see a hue still reads the line.
