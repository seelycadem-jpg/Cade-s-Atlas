# Design brief (Cade, 2026-11-05)

What Phase 1 designs against. In Cade's words, tidied.

## From Prototype 1

- The prototype was okay. The one thing to keep is **navigation centred on the globe**: the
  globe is the front door, and it opens the other pages (`docs/vision.md`, The front door).
- The feel read as an AI-made website: odd spacing; no use of pop-ups, floating panels or
  cards with their own edges; nothing that made it look clean.
- Tooltips and previews were missing where they were needed.
- The aesthetic looked like everything else. It needs a look of its own.

## What the new design must do

1. The globe is the centre of navigation, and every other place is reached from it.
2. Clean: deliberate spacing, panels and cards that float over the globe with their own
   edges, one obvious next action per screen.
3. Previews and tooltips everywhere a name, dot, marker or bar appears (the one shared
   preview card; a tap on a phone, hover on a laptop).
4. A look that is not the default AI look (the `frontend-design` skill lists the tells:
   cream background with a serif and a terracotta accent, acid-green on black, broadsheet
   hairlines, identical rounded cards, all-caps eyebrow labels, middle-dot meta strings).
   Prototype 1 sat close to the first of these; the new direction is chosen on purpose.

## Aesthetic lead (Cade, 2026-11-07)

A subtle **old European map** feeling: a quiet reference, never a costume. Keep the cues
that come from engraved charts (hairline coastlines, land in a paper tone, a serif for
place names on the globe and little else, a faint graticule, ink spent only where it
says something). Leave out the costume (parchment textures, sepia, cartouches, compass
roses, torn edges, fake aging). The interface around the map (bar, cards, reading type)
stays modern and clean, so the contrast reads as deliberate. Caution: cream plus serif
plus a red-brown accent is the commonest AI default and where Prototype 1 sat; the
restraint is the whole job. Step 1.3 shows one direction leaning this way and at least
one that does not.

## References

- **ATLYNX**: clean de facto borders for the present day. A reference for the look of
  borders and for the post-2019 record (roadmap step 4.13); its licence is checked before
  any data is used.
